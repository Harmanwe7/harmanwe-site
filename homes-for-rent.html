How Renting Works

Four steps from first look to move-in

The same process for every applicant, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Review the criteria

    See how we evaluate income, rental history and vouchers on our qualification page.

  2. Prequalify

    A short online form through RentRedi tells us you are ready to tour.

  3. Tour the home

    Showings are Wednesdays 4–6 PM and Saturdays 10 AM–1 PM. We confirm your time by call or email.

    Submit your application through RentRedi. Once approved, we sign the lease and hand you the keys.
