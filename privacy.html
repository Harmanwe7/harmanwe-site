Privacy Policy

How Harmanwe, LLC handles the information you share with us through harmanwe.com.

Effective October 26, 2026

Who we are

Harmanwe, LLC is a family-owned residential rental company located at 565 Cedar Road, Units 18 & 19, Chesapeake, Virginia 23322. In this policy, "Harmanwe," "we," and "us" refer to Harmanwe, LLC.

Information you choose to share

When you use the contact form on our website, we receive the information you enter:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide it
  • The reason for your inquiry and your message

Form messages are delivered to our business email inbox through an email delivery service. Our website does not store your message in a database. We keep inquiry emails as long as needed to respond to you and for our ordinary business records.

Information collected automatically

  • Visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to understand how many people visit our pages and how quickly they load. These tools report aggregated statistics such as page views, general location (city or region), browser, and device type. They do not use cookies and do not identify you personally.
  • Hosting records. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) processes technical information such as IP address and browser type to deliver pages and keep the site secure.
  • Fonts. Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address under Google's privacy policy.

Our website does not set cookies.

How we use your information

We use the information you share to respond to your inquiry, tell you about availability, and communicate with current residents. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and our email — such as our website host (Vercel), our email delivery service (Resend), and our email provider (Google Workspace) — or when required by law.

Rental applications and other websites

Rental applications, prequalification, rent payments, and maintenance requests are handled through RentRedi, not through this website. Information you provide there is governed by RentRedi's privacy policy. Our site may also link to listing sites such as Zillow, which have their own policies.

Children

Our website is intended for adults seeking housing. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Our site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect the information you share with us.

Your choices

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete inquiry emails you have sent us by contacting us at info@harmanwe.com or (757) 916-0466.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
